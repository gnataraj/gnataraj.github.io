Hi, I’m Nataraj 👋

I’ve spent close to two decades in software engineering, with the last several years focused on DevOps and cloud. Most of my work revolves around building scalable, reliable systems on AWS using tools like Kubernetes, Terraform, and CI/CD pipelines.

This blog is my personal space to share what I learn along the way — things that worked, things that didn’t, and lessons picked up from real-world projects. You’ll find posts on DevOps, cloud architecture, automation, and performance, all written from a hands-on, practical perspective.

I enjoy breaking down complex topics into simple, usable ideas, especially for engineers who are building and operating systems in production.

Lately, I’ve also been exploring areas like multi-cloud, security, and how AI is starting to shape DevOps.

All opinions expressed here are my own and do not reflect the views of my employer.
